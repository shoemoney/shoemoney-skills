## J. Don't drain a known backlog with this loop
The loop spends ~40 min on capture + two reviews *to discover* defects; backlog items are
already discovered. Recipe that worked: (1) one read-only agent per theme, no Godot — 3 of 4
backlog Confirms came back "not real"; (2) group survivors **by file, not by symptom** — that
is the unit the gate can accept or salvage, and exactly why `batch:3` failed; (3) one unit per
commit, serially, in a worktree.

## K. A fix that removes a harm must pin the good behaviour sharing the mechanism
*"It no longer hurts you"* is equally satisfied by making the thing useless. Claymore grace
shipped **three** assertions together: planter unhurt during grace, enemy still dies at tick 2
during grace, planter hurt again at tick 5 after. A one-sided check certifies a nerf.
